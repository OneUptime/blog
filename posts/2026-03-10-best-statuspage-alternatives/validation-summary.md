## Issues Found
- Atlassian Statuspage pricing was outdated and conflated plan types. Updated the text to reflect public page pricing: paid public plans start at $29/month, Business is $399/month, and Enterprise is $1,499/month.
- The Atlassian outage duration was stated as 21 days. Atlassian's post-incident review says the April 2022 outage spanned up to 14 days for a subset of affected customers, so the claim was corrected.
- OneUptime was described as MIT licensed. The official repository lists Apache 2.0, so the license references were corrected.
- Instatus was described as having no built-in monitoring. Its current product/pricing page includes monitors, checks, alerts, and on-call features, so the description and comparison table were updated.
- Better Stack's paid starting price was outdated. Updated the paid responder starting price to $29/user/month annually or $34/user/month monthly.
- Sorry pricing was outdated. Updated the starting price from $29/month to $99/month based on the current pricing page.
- Openstatus was described as MIT licensed. Its repository lists AGPL-3.0, so the license references were corrected.
- Status.io pricing was described as custom pricing only. Its public pricing page lists Basic at $79/month and Enterprise starting at $999/month, so the pricing claim and table were updated.
- Status.io was described as offering SOC 2 and multi-language support. I did not find those claims on the current official pricing/security pages, so the bullets were replaced with officially documented security and private-page capabilities.
- StatusPal was marked as having no built-in monitoring. Its pricing and feature text include monitored services and out-of-the-box monitoring, so the con wording and comparison table were corrected.

## Review Notes
Pricing and packaging for hosted SaaS products changes frequently. Future refreshes should re-check plan names, subscriber limits, and whether monitoring features are first-party checks, third-party integrations, or external service-status aggregation.
