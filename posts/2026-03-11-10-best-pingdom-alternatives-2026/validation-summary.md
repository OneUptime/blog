## Issues Found
- OneUptime was described as MIT licensed. Changed this to Apache 2.0 to match the current repository license.
- Datadog Synthetics was listed as "Custom" pricing with a limited free tier. Changed this to usage-based pricing and trial-only free access for synthetics.
- Checkly pricing was listed as starting at $30/month. Changed this to $24/month based on current published pricing.
- Checkly was described as having no incident management or status pages. Updated the limitation to note that status pages and basic incident communication exist, while full on-call scheduling does not.
- Better Stack was described as having no distributed tracing. Updated this to clarify that tracing is available, but Better Stack is not positioned as a full APM platform like Datadog or New Relic.
- Site24x7 was listed as trial-only and its server-monitoring pricing was stale. Updated the table to show a free tier and corrected the pricing text to reflect the $9/month all-in-one plan with limited server monitoring.
- Site24x7 was described as having 120+ monitoring locations. Updated this to 130+ global monitoring locations.
- Updown.io was described as HTTP(S)-only. Updated this to include HTTP(S), TCP, and ICMP checks.
- New Relic Synthetics pricing was described as consuming only the 100GB data allocation. Updated this to mention the free synthetic check allowance and separate billing for additional checks.
- Uptrends was listed as starting at $16/month. Updated this to the current $210/month Core plan.
- Uptrends browser support listed Firefox and PhantomJS. Updated this to current Chrome and Edge browser monitoring.
