## Issues Found
- Datadog integration count was outdated at 750+. Updated it to 1,000+ based on Datadog's documentation and October 2025 milestone announcement.
- OpenObserve was listed as Apache 2.0. Updated it to AGPLv3 to match the current repository license.
- Axiom pricing used older/free-tier wording and referred to "Pro." Updated it to distinguish the 500GB/month Personal data-loading limit from Axiom Cloud, which starts at $25/month and includes a larger always-free allowance.
- SigNoz Cloud pricing was listed as starting at $199/month. Updated it to $49/month based on SigNoz's current pricing page and pricing change announcement.
- Sumo Logic pricing listed a 500MB/day free tier and an approximate per-GB Essentials price. Updated it to the current 1GB/day free data-volume limit and credit-based paid-plan wording.

## Review Notes
Pricing and packaging for observability vendors changes frequently. The corrected wording avoids overly specific per-GB claims where the vendor's current official pricing model is credit-based or plan-based.
