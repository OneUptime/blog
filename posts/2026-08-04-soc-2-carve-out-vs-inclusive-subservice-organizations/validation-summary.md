## Review Notes
The post contains no executable code, commands, or machine-readable configuration. It was reviewed as a technical compliance guide because it includes a concrete dependency-record template and operational procedures for classifying subservice organizations, documenting controls, and reviewing provider assurance. The distinctions between carve-out and inclusive treatment, CSOCs and CUECs, and Type I dates versus Type II periods are consistent with the consulted AICPA materials. AWS and Google Cloud documentation also supports the guidance to verify service scope, report periods, and bridge or continued-operations material rather than relying on a report's cover page alone.
