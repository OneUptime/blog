## Issues Found
- The post claimed Flux post-renderers can modify "any resource generated by a Helm chart" and provide "complete control." Flux documents a Helm limitation where post-renderers are not applied to chart hooks. I updated the wording to scope the claim to Helm-rendered non-hook manifests.
- The StatefulSet example targeted every rendered StatefulSet by kind and used `metadata.name: placeholder` in the strategic merge patch. While Kustomize can patch multiple resources this way, the example is safer and more accurate as a targeted resource override. I changed the target and patch metadata name to `elasticsearch`.
