## Issues Found

- The BYOD privacy guidance said to obtain consent for every selected device model. This could imply that worker consent is always the appropriate legal basis for device management or monitoring. Employment privacy guidance notes that consent may be invalid or inappropriate where workers lack a genuine choice. The sentence now requires an appropriate legal basis and limits consent to circumstances where it is required and valid.

## Review Notes

- The post correctly presents its workforce tiers and safeguards as risk-based design examples rather than mandatory SOC 2 requirements.
- NIST SP 800-46 Rev. 2 remains the current final revision; NIST's Rev. 3 item is an initial preliminary draft, not a replacement final publication.
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 has a Release 5.2.0 update dated August 27, 2025. The account management, remote access, external-system, personnel-screening, and personnel-termination guidance relied upon by this post remains applicable.
- The post contains no executable code, CLI commands, or configuration syntax to validate.
