## Issues Found
- The basic combined example said the VirtualService defined three routing rules, but the YAML defines two HTTP route entries: one matched route for `end-user: jason` and one default weighted route. Changed the prose to "two routing rules."
- The fault injection section said DestinationRule outlier detection ejects endpoints from the injected faults. Istio abort faults are generated by the proxy and returned to the downstream caller, while DestinationRule outlier detection is for unhealthy upstream hosts in the load balancing pool. Updated the explanation to say the injected faults test callers, and outlier detection applies to real consecutive 5xx responses.
- The troubleshooting section said short host names and fully qualified service names should resolve to the same service without a namespace caveat. Istio resolves short names relative to the namespace of the rule. Clarified that `my-service` and `my-service.default.svc.cluster.local` match when the resources are in the `default` namespace.
