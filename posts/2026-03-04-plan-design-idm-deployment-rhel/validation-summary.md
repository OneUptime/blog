## Issues Found
- The topology example called the first server "primary." IdM uses multiple read/write replica replication, so this was changed to "initial server" to avoid implying a permanent primary role.
- The external DNS example listed only a subset of the records needed for an IdM deployment without integrated DNS. It was updated to show the LDAP, Kerberos master, Kerberos TCP/UDP, kpasswd TCP/UDP, and realm TXT records generated by `ipa-server-install`.
- The sizing guidance said "minimum hardware" and listed 8 GB for 10,000+ users. It was updated to match Red Hat's current RAM guidance: at least 4 GB RAM plus 4 GB swap for 10,000 users and 100 groups, and at least 16 GB RAM plus 4 GB swap for 100,000 users and 50,000 groups.
- The firewall example used older individual FreeIPA firewalld services. It was updated to use the current `freeipa-4` service and to open `dns` only when integrated DNS is used.
- The port list omitted UDP for Kerberos and DNS. The descriptions now explicitly note TCP and UDP where required.
- The final note said changing the CA type after deployment requires complete reinstallation. Current Red Hat documentation supports adding CA services to an existing CA-less deployment and switching between self-signed and externally signed integrated CA configurations, so the statement was revised to say realm changes require reinstallation while CA changes require careful planning.

## Review Notes
The guide is intentionally high level. Future improvements could mention IPv6 kernel requirements, chrony/time synchronization, and the current Red Hat-supported maximum of 60 replicas in one IdM domain, but those omissions do not make the existing guidance incorrect.
